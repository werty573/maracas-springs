Terms and delivery

Last updated 2 November 2026

Ordering

Add a pack to your cart and press "Place order". Your order opens in WhatsApp, and it is confirmed once we reply with your total and delivery time. We may decline or limit an order, for example when stock is low.

Prices

All prices are in Trinidad and Tobago dollars (TT$). A 500ml bottle is TT$10. Packs are priced at TT$10 a bottle. Prices can change, but the price we confirm on WhatsApp is the price you pay.

Payment

  • Cash on delivery: pay the driver when your water arrives.
  • Bank transfer: we send our bank details on WhatsApp, and your order goes out once the transfer clears.

We will never ask for your card number or online banking password on WhatsApp.

Delivery and pickup

We deliver across Trinidad. The delivery fee depends on your area and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay. You can also collect your order for free on North Coast Road, Maracas Bay, at a time we agree with you.

Damaged or wrong orders

If a bottle arrives damaged, leaking or the order is wrong, message us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo and we will replace it at no cost. For food safety, we cannot take back bottles that have been opened, except when there is a problem with the water itself.

About the water

Our lab results come from KaizenLAB, an A2LA accredited laboratory, and refer to the samples tested (report dated 9 October 2026). Store bottles in a cool place out of direct sunlight.
