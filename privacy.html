Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who we are

Maracas Springs sells natural spring water from Maracas, Trinidad and Tobago. You can reach us on WhatsApp at 868-724-8356 or by email at sureshseecharan@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you check out, you type your name, WhatsApp number, delivery address and payment choice. This website does not store those details on a server. Pressing "Place order" opens WhatsApp with your order written out, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

Once you send it, we keep your order messages in WhatsApp so we can confirm, deliver and follow up on your order. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to the messages you send through it.

Storage on your device

We use your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to keep:

  • your cart, so it is still there if you leave and come back
  • your light or dark theme choice
  • your cookie choice, so we do not ask again

These are needed for the shop to work and never leave your device.

Analytics

If you choose "Accept", we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not tell us who you are. If you choose "Essential only", it does not load. You can change your choice any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Hosting and security

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic connection data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and block attacks. All pages are served over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We only share your address and phone number with the person delivering your order.

How long we keep it

We keep order messages for as long as we need them for delivery, customer service and our business records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

In line with the Data Protection Act of Trinidad and Tobago, you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us on WhatsApp or by email and we will respond.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
